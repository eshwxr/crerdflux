What it does

Three guardrails between a model and production.

Most ML systems retrain on a schedule and deploy on hope. CredFlux replaces hope with three checks that every candidate model has to pass.

Drift monitoring

A PSI + KS-test monitor scores all 19 features of every incoming batch against the training distribution. Tuned to catch real shifts while keeping quiet weeks quiet — 91% detection at a 9% false-alarm rate across 90 simulated scenarios.

Shadow-mode promotion

Drift triggers a retrain, but the challenger never touches live traffic. It scores the same requests silently and gets promoted only if it beats the champion by a full point of AUC. Anything less is churn, not improvement.

Fairness gate

Before any promotion, false-positive rates are compared across age bands. A challenger that is more accurate but more biased gets blocked — and the decision is written to a permanent audit log.

Why it matters

Accuracy is not a license for bias.

In one recorded run, a challenger beat its champion by +7.65% AUC — and CredFlux still refused to ship it. Its false-positive rate for one age band was 22 points higher than another's. The gate caught it, blocked it, and logged why.

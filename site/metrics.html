The strongest result

The gate that said no.

A challenger with +7.65% AUC over its champion was blocked because its false-positive rate for the 40–59 age band was 22.4 points higher than for under-25s. After per-group threshold calibration brought the disparity down to 4.4 points, the same challenger passed both gates and was promoted. The gate catches real bias — and it isn't a dead code path.
